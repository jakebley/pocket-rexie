# Playdates with friends' pets (future feature, not scheduled)

Idea: your pet can visit a friend's pet, and the two spend time together.

## Constraints to settle before building
- No accounts or servers at launch. A first version can be local: two phones nearby (Multipeer), or a shared code or link that carries a small, signed pet snapshot (species, stage, name, mood).
- Children's privacy: no free-text chat, no location, no real names. Pets only.
- Effects should be gentle: a playdate gives happiness and a Dex "met" entry, never harms the pet.
- Plus tie-in: seeing a friend's Plus-family pet lets you view it, not own it (a reason to upgrade).
- Pairs naturally with the collection profile (docs/COLLECTION.md): "met 12 species".

## Order
After the collection profile. Needs the shared Dex store first.
