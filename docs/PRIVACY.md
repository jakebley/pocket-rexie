# Privacy Policy - Pocket Rexie

_Last updated: 2026-10-21_

Pocket Rexie does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

- **Storage:** Your pet's state is stored only on your device, in an App Group container shared between the app, its widgets and the Watch app. It never leaves your device.
- **Notifications:** Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push server or remote notification service is used. You can turn them off in iOS Settings.
- **No accounts, ads, analytics or third-party SDKs.**
- **Health data:** The app does not use HealthKit and does not read any health or fitness data.
- **Children:** The app collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Deleting the app removes its data. Contact: add your support email here before publishing.
