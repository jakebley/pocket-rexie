# Art polish brief (one family per agent)

Goal: a critical re-look and final polish of every species in ONE family. The owner says some are "really off". Fix those, do not just tweak colours.

## Setup
- `git fetch origin && git worktree add -B polish-<family> /home/user/wt-polish-<family> origin/main`, work only there. `expo-prototype/node_modules`: symlink to /home/user/PocketRexie/expo-prototype/node_modules or `npm ci`; never commit node_modules.
- You may edit only: `tools/archetypes_<family>.py`, `tools/families/<family>.py` (names and blurbs, decorations used by species, LINE_COLORS, COLOR_OVERRIDES), `tools/reactions.py` (only the lines for your family's kinds), `docs/families/<family>*.md|png`, and family-specific biome art if a species needs it. Do NOT change species ids, the species count, stages, EVO requirements, or any other family's files.
- Generated files (`Shared/SpeciesData.swift`, `expo-prototype/src/core/speciesData.ts`, `reactionsData.ts`) are regenerated by the coordinator at merge time. Run the generators locally to test, but do NOT commit generated files (`git checkout -- Shared/SpeciesData.swift expo-prototype/src/core/speciesData.ts expo-prototype/src/core/reactionsData.ts` before committing).

## The standard
1. Recognisable at a glance at real in-app size: render each species in Technicolor on its own biome (tools/shading-family.sh, tools/shading-sheets.js, tools/biome-sheets.js) and LOOK at it, not only at the plain-ink 16x16 sheet. A stranger should name the animal or thing without the label.
2. Distinct silhouettes: no two species in the family at the same or adjacent stage that differ only by one decoration or size. Write a small script that computes the pixel overlap of each pair of 16x16 sprites (frame 0) and lists pairs above about 85% overlap, then fix those by changing the body (proportions, limbs, ears, tail, pose, head shape), not by adding a decoration.
3. Stage growth reads: baby < child < teen < adult < ultimate in size and complexity, and each line has a visible character (strays look scruffy or dim, ultimates look special).
4. Clean pixels: no stray single pixels, no broken limbs, no heads fused to bodies, legs at least one pixel wide with clear gaps, a readable face with an eye hole near the head (the eye is a hole; the renderer fills it in Technicolor). The blink frame must differ only in the eye.
5. Check the smoothed 32x32 version (Scale2x) too: thin features that vanish after smoothing need thickening.
6. Keep the lit pixel set legal for Traditional: it is the same sprite, drawn as ink. Do not add busy detail that turns to noise in one colour.
7. Species names and blurbs: fix anything that misdescribes the art after your changes; keep names unique across all families.
